# 5 Easy-to-Create Weight Loss Digital Products

*Based on analysis of 60 Etsy listings ("digital products weight loss"), ~285 positive and 90 low-rated (1–3★) reviews — October 2026.*

## The pain point behind most bad reviews

**Overwhelm plus things that break:** too many pages, links and formulas that stop working, files that won't open on Android, and US-only setup.

## Rules for all five products

- **Start Here page:** one page you can set up in 2 minutes; advanced pages stay optional.
- **Works anywhere:** undated, kg and lbs, EU and US dates.
- **Tested before listing:** on Android with free Xodo or Samsung Notes, on iPad, and printed. Say "tested on Android" in the listing.
- **Short video walkthrough:** 2–3 minutes, linked from page 1.
- **No filler:** a page count you can defend, no padding with cover pages.

---

## 1. "First 10 kg" Visual Progress Tracker (printable + tablet PDF)

- **Trend/hook:** "60 Pounds Lost" style color-in charts and milestone rewards (MyLifePlans ~196k sales, itsawhoot ~64k).
- **Pain solved:** overwhelm and US-only setup. 5 pages total. Versions in 5/10/20/30 kg and 10/25/50/100 lb. Boxes large enough to write in when printed.
- **Contents:**
  - Color-in kg/lb chart
  - Reward ladder ("at −5 kg I get…")
  - Measurements page
  - Before/after page
  - Grayscale version for e-ink tablets
- **Build:** Canva, ~1 day.
- **Price:** €3–5.
- **Title idea:** *Weight Loss Tracker Printable – Kg & Lbs – Color In Chart – Rewards – Undated – Android & iPad*

## 2. Simple-Mode Weight Loss Spreadsheet (Google Sheets + Excel)

- **Trend/hook:** the bestselling format (SimplyOrganizedPro ~110k sales, HayeCreativesCo ~96k).
- **Pain solved:** "too complex", "errors", "locked sheets", "current weight doesn't work", no net calories.
- **Contents:**
  - Tab 1 "Quick": date and weight only, auto chart
  - Tab 2 optional "Pro": calories eaten − burned = net, macros
  - Tab 3: measurements
  - Cells unlocked, plus a protected "restore" copy
  - Every error message explained on a help tab
- **Build:** Google Sheets, 1–2 days. Test on phone and desktop.
- **Price:** €6–9.
- **Hook:** "No app, no subscription, set up in 2 minutes."

## 3. GLP-1 Journey Tracker (Sheets + printable)

- **Trend/hook:** fastest-growing niche. GLP-1 marketing templates sell for €21–71, showing demand; few trackers exist for users themselves.
- **Pain solved:** buyers asked for journaling space and tracking of what matters on GLP-1 instead of food logs.
- **Contents:**
  - Dose and injection-site log
  - Weekly weigh-in plus auto chart
  - Side-effect and mood journal
  - Protein and water targets checklist
  - "Questions for my doctor" page
- **Disclaimer:** tracking only, no medical claims; add a short disclaimer.
- **Build:** 1–2 days (reuses most of #2).
- **Price:** €8–12.

## 4. 28-Day Kickstart Plan (ebook PDF + checklist)

- **Trend/hook:** day-count programs ("4-Week", "21-Day", "75 Soft") and niche audiences (after 40, busy moms).
- **Pain solved:** "more title pages than content", outdated PLR, AI filler.
- **Contents:**
  - 1 page of real guidance per week
  - Daily 3-habit checklist (protein, steps, water)
  - Budget meal list using affordable healthy foods (links to your portal's products)
  - Grocery list
  - Optional "After 40" and "Busy Mom" editions (same core, different intro and examples)
- **Build:** Canva, 2–3 days. Write it yourself; use AI for drafts only.
- **Price:** €5–8.

## 5. All-in-One Planner Lite (tablet PDF that works on Android)

- **Trend/hook:** the GoodNotes/iPad "all-in-one" planner (PlanRightCo ~89k sales, GirlGotGoals ~19k).
- **Pain solved:** this format collects the most 1★ reviews — broken links, blank pages, "not compatible with Samsung", "needs a $35/yr app", "overwhelming", pre-dated months.
- **Contents:**
  - 12 page types, not 300
  - Undated
  - Few hyperlinks, each one tested
  - "Which app should I use?" page recommending free apps
  - Printable fallback
- **Build:** Canva + PDF linking, 2 days.
- **Price:** €4–7.
- **Hook:** "Works on Android, iPad & paper. Free apps only."
